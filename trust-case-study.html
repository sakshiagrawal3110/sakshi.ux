Investment Intelligence

Designing trust in the age of AI

A confidential client investment technology and portfolio analytics platform designed to bring portfolio information, investment managers, documents and intelligent insights into one secure experience for institutional investors.

The platform was designed to give clients a single place to understand their investments, access supporting information and interact with their portfolio data-while maintaining the level of trust expected when working with highly sensitive financial information.

Confidential project: Product name, proprietary data, financial figures and production screens have been anonymised.

Impact

One secure experience

Designed around trust, making complex financial data scannable, and introducing AI without replacing professional expertise.

Problem

More information didn't mean a better experience.

Investment professionals were accustomed to working across multiple sources to access information about their portfolios, investment managers and supporting documents. The opportunity was to bring these experiences together into one secure platform.

But consolidation introduced a new problem. The platform could potentially contain an enormous amount of financial information. Simply putting everything in one place would create another fragmented experience-just inside a single product. The challenge became: How might we make complex investment information easier to understand without taking away the depth that professionals need?

Turning Point

Trust had to come before intelligence.

Early UserTesting helped us understand an important barrier. Users were interested in the value of having their information consolidated, but there was hesitation around putting sensitive investment data into a single digital environment.

The response became more positive when users could see how the platform protected their information, rather than simply being told that it was secure. This led to an important design principle: Don't ask users to trust the AI before they trust the platform.

Product Strategy

AI that assists, rather than decides.

AI was intentionally introduced as an augmentation layer rather than the centre of the product. Through interviews with investment managers and subject-matter experts, we identified areas where AI could reduce effort without interfering with professional judgement.

The interaction model remained deliberately human-led: AI surfaces information → User evaluates it → Professional applies judgement. This distinction was especially important in an environment where users were dealing with highly consequential financial information.

Fund Profile

Designing depth without overwhelming the user

One of the most complex areas of the product was the fund profile. The initial business instinct was understandably to expose as much information as possible. But more information created more cognitive load.

We restructured the fund profile around hierarchy, scannability and progressive disclosure. Instead of presenting financial information as large blocks of text, we introduced stronger visual cues that allowed users to understand the overall picture before diving deeper.

The Result

From fragmented information to one trusted experience.

The platform brought portfolio information, investment-manager details, documents, analytics and AI-assisted capabilities into one unified client experience.

The experience evolved from a potentially overwhelming collection of financial information into a structured platform where users could access the depth they needed without having to process everything at once.

In financial UX, trust isn't a feature. It's part of the experience.

Designing Trust: The Short Version

  • The Problem: Institutional investors needed a single platform for their portfolio data, but consolidating dense financial information created overwhelming cognitive load and trust barriers.
  • The Solution: We designed a secure, hierarchical experience that proved platform security upfront and utilized progressive disclosure to make dense financial data highly scannable.
  • AI Strategy: We implemented AI strictly as an augmentation layer (search and summarization) that assists rather than replaces professional financial judgment.
