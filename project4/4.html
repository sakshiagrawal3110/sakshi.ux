Synthesis summary
Across 47 observations from 12 participants, 5 core themes emerged around AI trust: Understanding, Verification, Control, Uncertainty, and Responsibility.
Key behavioural insight
Trust isn't binary - it's a spectrum from Accept → Question → Verify → Modify → Reject. Different contexts require different levels of user control and transparency.
Design implication
The goal isn't to make users trust AI more. It's to help users know when AI deserves their trust - through evidence, transparency, and meaningful control.
