Research → Product - translation framework

From research findings to design principles to product implications
5 findings · 5 opportunities · 5 principles · 5 implications
🔬 Research finding 5
01
Users verify high-stakes information extensively, but accept low-stakes output more readily.
02
Confidence isn't the same as credibility - polished, confident responses can trigger skepticism.
03
Users trust AI for discovery and organisation, but hesitate when AI moves toward recommendation and action.
04
Control is a trust mechanism - users want to inspect → modify → verify → reject, not just accept.
05
Uncertainty should be visible - honest uncertainty is more trustworthy than fake confidence.
▶ so
🎯 Opportunity 5
01
Design for contextual transparency - surface evidence at the moment of decision, not earlier.
02
Replace confidence signalling with evidence signalling - show why, not just what.
03
Match transparency to risk - low-stakes = quick answer, high-stakes = full context.
04
Build control into the experience - make inspection and modification first-class interactions.
05
Normalise uncertainty - tell users what AI doesn't know, not just what it knows.
▶ then
✧ Design principle 5
01
Show evidence, not confidence - make AI output verifiable, not just convincing.
02
Give users an escape hatch - always allow users to inspect → edit → reject.
03
Match transparency to risk - low-stakes = lightweight, high-stakes = comprehensive.
04
Make uncertainty visible - don't hide uncertainty behind polished language.
05
AI should assist the decision - support human judgement, don't obscure it.
▶ finally
🚀 Product implication 5
01
Surface evidence at the moment of decision - contextual, not overwhelming.
02
Build verification into the flow - make checking AI output fast and contextual.
03
Adapt the interface to risk - simple for low-stakes, detailed for high-stakes.
04
Expose the inputs - let users change criteria and see how output changes.
05
Communicate uncertainty clearly - show confidence intervals, not false precision.
The core insight
The goal isn't to make users trust AI more. It's to help users know when AI deserves their trust.
The design imperative
Design for verification, not blind acceptance. Build control into the experience.
The product shift
From “Trust our AI” to “Here's why you can trust this” - with evidence you can verify.
