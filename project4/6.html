Key insight 01
Trust isn't binary - it's a spectrum of behaviours from acceptance to rejection
Key insight 02
Higher stakes shift users toward verification and rejection, demanding more control
Key insight 03
The goal isn't to push users to accept - it's to support them at every point on the spectrum
AI Explorers: more likely to accept AI Pragmatists: balanced across spectrum AI Skeptics: more likely to question & verify
