FAQs

Common questions from investment professionals evaluating the platform.

How often is portfolio and manager data refreshed?+

Portfolio values, allocations, and manager performance figures update on a daily basis, with source documents refreshed as new filings and commentary become available.

Can I export a fund or manager view as a report?+

Any fund profile, manager summary, or document set can be exported as a PDF for offline review or client sharing directly from its detail screen.

How is my portfolio information secured?+

Data is encrypted in transit and at rest, and every session requires verified authentication - the same protections carried through from the platform's secure access flow.

Does the AI summary replace my own analysis?+

No - every AI-generated summary or search result links back to its source document, so it supports your judgement rather than substituting for it.

Who can I contact if a manager or fund is missing?+

Reach the platform support team through the Help menu in the header, available from any screen.
